# 3. Project Mission & SMART Goals

## Mission Statement

The mission of the EthiViz V9 operational evidence initiative is to strengthen service reliability and governance while preserving the system's exploratory status until independent, qualified evaluation supports any stronger analytical claims.

## Strategic Mission Outcomes

- Establish whether V9 meets a deployment-specific operational release gate.
- Preserve transparency about exploratory analysis and unvalidated outputs.
- Measure service behavior and document unmeasured capacity and retention boundaries.
- Define accountable ownership and governance before any broader deployment.

## SMART Goals

### Goal 1: Evidence-Based Deployment Decision

- Specific: Assess each supported V9 deployment against technical, privacy, accessibility, and governance criteria.
- Measurable: Every criterion is backed by evidence or explicitly marked open; no open item is silently treated as passed.
- Achievable: Use the V9 implementation, CI tests, benchmark reports, and deployment-specific reviews.
- Relevant: Prevents version delivery from being mistaken for production approval.
- Time-bound: Before any deployment decision.

### Goal 2: Security Hardening

- Specific: Implement and validate security controls for data handling, authentication/authorization, deployment environment, and operational monitoring.
- Measurable: 100% of prioritized security control gaps closed or formally accepted with mitigation plans.
- Achievable: Benchmark against defined secure architecture and deployment standards.
- Relevant: Essential for trust, compliance, and safe production use.
- Time-bound: Before any external exposure.

### Goal 3: Operational Reliability

- Specific: Measure availability, queue behavior, resource use, and recovery for each intended deployment profile.
- Measurable: Publish tested limits and sample counts; mark unavailable metrics as unmeasured.
- Achievable: Through repeatable API benchmarks, failure tests, and deployment runbooks.
- Relevant: Protects user continuity and organizational credibility.
- Time-bound: Within the production readiness window.

### Goal 4: Quality Assurance & Acceptance

- Specific: Validate functional, non-functional, and user acceptance criteria with a defined sign-off process.
- Measurable: All critical and high-severity defects resolved before release, with no unresolved blocking issues.
- Achievable: Through test planning, regression coverage, and approval gates.
- Relevant: Supports trust, usability, and product integrity.
- Time-bound: Prior to final release decision.

### Goal 5: Governance & Stakeholder Alignment

- Specific: Establish a governance structure with defined decision rights, reporting cadence, and escalation procedures.
- Measurable: 100% of milestones tracked and all steering decisions logged.
- Achievable: Supported by PMO processes, RACI, and meeting cadence.
- Relevant: Enables consistent decision-making and accountability.
- Time-bound: Implement at project start and maintain throughout lifecycle.

## Success Measures

The program is successful when:

- release criteria are evidenced or explicitly held open
- no unresolved critical security or reliability blocker is silently accepted
- stakeholder communication is timely and consistent
- the project remains within approved scope, budget, and schedule baselines
- operational ownership is formally assigned across engineering, security, and support

## Mission Alignment

This mission aligns with responsible AI practice by balancing innovation with evidence, accountability, and clear use limitations. Project artifacts are not proof that named owners, operating controls, or independent review are in place.
