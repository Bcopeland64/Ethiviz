# 8. Communication Plan

## Purpose

The communication plan ensures that all stakeholders receive timely, accurate, and relevant information about Ethiviz_V6 production-readiness progress, risks, decisions, and launch status. Clear communication reduces uncertainty and protects stakeholder confidence.

## Stakeholder Groups

| Stakeholder Group | Interest | Information Need | Communication Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor | strategic value, budget, major decisions | progress, risks, approval needs | steering committee, dashboard, ad hoc briefings |
| Product Owner | product readiness and scope | priorities, status, defects, acceptance | weekly meetings, issue trackers |
| Technical Team | delivery and technical issues | build status, defects, technical risk | daily stand-ups, sprint reviews |
| Security and Compliance | control posture | security findings, mitigations, risk status | targeted reviews and issue logs |
| QA and Validation | quality status | test results, defects, release readiness | quality review sessions |
| Operations | production support readiness | monitoring, runbooks, launch support | readiness reviews, deployment checklists |
| End Users / Internal Stakeholders | usability and value | release milestones, known limitations | email updates, onboarding guidance |

## Communication Cadence

| Communication | Frequency | Owner | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily stand-up | Daily | PM/Tech Lead | delivery team |
| Weekly status review | Weekly | PM | sponsor and leaders |
| Risk review | Weekly or biweekly | PM/risks owner | governance stakeholders |
| Steering committee update | Biweekly/monthly | PM | sponsor and senior stakeholders |
| QA release readiness review | Prior to go-live | QA Lead | sponsor, PM, technical, security |
| Operational readiness review | Prior to go-live | Operations Lead | sponsor, PM, technical leadership |

## Communication Channels

- project status reports
- steering committee decks
- issue and risk registers
- meeting minutes and action logs
- release readiness checklist
- email and chat updates for fast-turn blockers

## Message Standards

- status updates must be factual and time-bound
- blockers must be named, owned, and escalated when necessary
- risks and issues must be separated clearly
- all decisions must be recorded in the project log

## Communication Success Criteria

The communication plan is effective when stakeholders know the project status, can understand trade-offs, and are not surprised by delivery changes or risks. The project should foster confidence through transparency and consistency.
