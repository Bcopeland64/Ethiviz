# 5. Governance & Milestone Framework

## Governance Model

Ethiviz_V6 will be governed through a lightweight but formal project structure designed to support fast decisions without sacrificing accountability. The structure consists of:

- Executive Sponsor: final strategic owner and decision authority
- Steering Committee: monthly/biweekly high-level governance forum
- Project Manager: delivery coordinator, schedule owner, reporting lead
- Product Owner: business requirement and prioritization lead
- Technical Lead: engineering direction and solution integrity
- Security Lead: risk, design review, and control validation
- QA Lead: quality gate and release acceptance
- Operations Lead: production readiness and support transition

## Governance Cadence

| Forum | Frequency | Purpose | Attendees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daily stand-up | Daily | Delivery status and blockers | PM, technical leads, development team |
| Weekly project review | Weekly | Progress, risk, scope, action plan | PM, product owner, technical leads |
| Security review | Biweekly | Control validation and risk review | Security lead, technical lead, PM |
| Steering committee | Biweekly or monthly | Strategic alignment, decisions, budget/scope issues | Sponsor, PM, product owner, key leaders |
| Go/no-go review | As needed before release | Production approval | Sponsor, PM, technical, QA, security, operations |

## Decision Rights

- Business scope changes require sponsor or steering committee approval.
- Technical design changes require technical lead plus PM review, with security implications escalated appropriately.
- Release approval requires quality sign-off, operational readiness confirmation, and risk acceptance review.
- Risk acceptance above pre-defined threshold requires sponsor sign-off.

## Milestone Framework

| Phase | Milestone | Target Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Initiation | Charter approved | Scope, objectives, governance, and responsibilities established |
| Discovery & planning | Requirements baseline and plan approved | Functional and non-functional requirements validated |
| Design & build | Technical design approved and development underway | Architecture decisions and implementation track are stable |
| Integration & validation | System integrated and test cycle underway | Key defects resolved; performance and quality checks active |
| Production readiness review | Readiness gate completed | Security, deployment, operations, and support checks closed |
| Release approval | Go/no-go decision | Final sponsor approval before rollout |
| Stabilization | Hypercare period | Problems addressed, monitoring tuned, release confirmed |
| Transition to BAU | Operational handover | Ownership, support model, and reporting are embedded in routine operations |

## Milestone Control Principles

- Milestones are tied to decision gates, not only task completion.
- Each milestone must have evidence of completion.
- Delays require documented impact assessment and corrective action.
- All milestone changes must be reflected in the project baseline and communicated to stakeholders.

## Governance Success Criteria

The governance framework is effective when decisions are timely, risks are visible, and the program stays aligned to scope, quality, and business priorities without uncontrolled drift.
