# 10. Risk Management Plan

## Purpose

This plan describes how risks will be identified, assessed, prioritized, mitigated, monitored, and reported during the Ethiviz_V6 production-readiness effort.

## Risk Management Objectives

- detect risks early before they become major delivery issues
- align risk response with business, technical, and operational impact
- ensure ownership and accountability for each major risk
- maintain a current risk register as a live management tool

## Risk Categories

- technical and architecture risk
- security and compliance risk
- quality and defect risk
- schedule and dependency risk
- stakeholder and adoption risk
- operational and support risk

## Risk Assessment Method

Each risk will be evaluated using:

- probability (low, medium, high)
- impact (low, medium, high)
- urgency / trigger point
- owner and response plan

Risk priority will be determined by impact and likelihood; critical risks should be escalated to the sponsor or steering committee.

## Risk Response Strategies

| Strategy | Use When | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Avoid | risk cannot be tolerated | remove or redesign risky feature path |
| Mitigate | risk is manageable with planned controls | add test coverage, hardening, or controls |
| Transfer | risk is best handled by a specialist or vendor | external security validation |
| Accept | risk is within tolerance | minor non-critical operational risk |

## Risk Review Cadence

- weekly review by PM and core team
- biweekly focus review with security and technical leadership
- monthly steering review for high-severity items

## Escalation Rules

- High-impact risks require sponsor awareness and documented action.
- Unresolved critical risks cannot proceed to release approval without explicit risk acceptance.
- Any risk affecting scope, quality, or security must be logged immediately.

## Risk Management Success Criteria

The project is managing risk effectively when major threats are visible, owned, and mitigated before they affect schedule, cost, or trust. A transparent risk culture is essential for production confidence.
